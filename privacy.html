Privacy policy

HQ time backups · last updated 9 November 2026

Who uses it

This app is used by one person, its owner, to back up their own data to their own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses in Google Drive

Only the files and folder the app creates itself (the drive.file permission). It cannot see, read or change any other file in the Drive. It does not use any other Google data: no email, contacts, calendar or profile information beyond what Google shows on its own sign-in page.

What it uploads

Backups of the app's own time-tracking database. Each backup is encrypted on the owner's device before it is uploaded, with a key only the owner holds, so the files in Drive cannot be read without that key.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with, sold to or shown to anyone. There are no ads, analytics or third parties. The sign-in token is stored encrypted on the owner's device and is used only to upload and tidy up the backups.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the app in its Settings page or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backups already in Drive stay there until the owner deletes them.
